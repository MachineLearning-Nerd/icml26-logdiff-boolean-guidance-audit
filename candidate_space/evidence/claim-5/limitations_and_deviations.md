# Claim 5 limitations and deviations

- The certificate is exhaustive over three finite independent-product domains
  and one complete eight-leaf taxonomy, not a machine-checked proof for
  arbitrary domain cardinalities.
- Compilation is checked at the semantic-event level against an independent
  full-joint oracle.
- The invalid-overlap control is outside the theorem's taxonomy assumptions and
  is expected to fail; it demonstrates fail-closed behavior rather than a
  counterexample.
