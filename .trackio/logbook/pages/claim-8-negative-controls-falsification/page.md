Three fail-closed controls are tested. CI conjunction and CI disjunction are deliberately applied to correlated events; both disagree with the exact joint oracle. The constant 0.5 score mixture is applied to a mutually-exclusive OR and also disagrees. All 3/3 invalid constructions are rejected; the smallest detected error is 0.0590.
