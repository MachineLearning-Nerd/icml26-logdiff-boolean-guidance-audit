# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Francesco Alesiani, Jonathan Warrell, Tanja Bien, Henrik
Christiansen, Matheus Ferraz, and Mathias Niepert for developing LOGDIFF and
for making the paper, official implementation, and experiment context
available to the research community.

Your work makes a difficult question—when logical compositions of diffusion
guidance are exact—concrete enough to inspect, test, and discuss. This
repository is an independent reproduction audit built around your public paper
and the pinned public source release. It records both successful finite
certificates and the places where the public release does not yet support a
faithful full-scale reproduction.

The thank-you is appreciation, not an assertion of collaboration, review, or
author endorsement. Please use the paper's own citation when building on the
method.
