# Claim 5 method

For independent groups, the complete domains `(2,2,2)`, `(3,2)`, and `(3,3)`
contain 254, 62, and 510 nonconstant events. Every event is compiled into CI
conjunctions joined by ME disjunctions and compared to a full-joint oracle over
25 posterior settings: 20,650 events total.

For a balanced eight-leaf taxonomy with 15 predicates, every one of the 254
nonconstant leaf events is compiled and compared over 25 settings: 6,350 events
total. An overlapping predicate pair with neither nesting nor mutual exclusion
is deliberately sent through the ME rule and must be rejected.
