# Claim 5 source audit

The source retrieval and SHA-256 are recorded in Claim 1's source audit.
Proposition C.2 is anchored at `#A3.SS2.4`. It states completeness in the sense
of compilability for two distribution classes: collections of conditionally
independent categorical variables, and properties expressible in a taxonomy.
For the taxonomy class, terminal-time structure is stated to propagate to all
time points under the paper's assumptions.

The executable certificate is exhaustive over its complete finite domains. It
does not generalize the finite enumeration into a proof for unbounded domains.
